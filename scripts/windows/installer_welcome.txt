Welcome to Document Studio setup.

This installer copies the app and bundled document tools into one folder — no terminal commands and no separate engine downloads for a full build.

Included when built as a complete release:
  • Document Studio (PDF viewer and tools)
  • qpdf — merge, split, compress, encrypt, decrypt
  • Tesseract OCR and language data
  • OpenSSL and NSS tools for digital signatures
  • ffmpeg for media-related tools
  • LibreOffice (optional in some builds) for high-fidelity Office to PDF

You need roughly 500 MB–1.5 GB of free disk space depending on the build.

Click Next to choose where to install, then Finish to launch Document Studio.

For updates, run a newer Setup.exe or use Help → About in the app.
