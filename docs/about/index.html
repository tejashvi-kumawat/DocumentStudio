About

About

Document Studio is an offline, privacy-first PDF and document workspace.

Built by Tejashvi Kumawat.

What it is

A real desktop app (Windows · macOS · Linux) for everyday PDF work — merge, split, compress, encrypt, OCR, Office convert, markup, and digital signatures — without uploading your files to a cloud service.

Principles

  • Documents stay on your device
  • No account wall, no ads
  • Honest security UX (no password cracking, real redaction)
  • Desktop releases bundle the engines they need
