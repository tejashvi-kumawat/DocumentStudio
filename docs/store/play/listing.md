## Full description

```
Document Studio is an offline, privacy-first workspace for PDFs and images.

Work on your device — no account, no cloud upload of your documents.

What you can do
• Open and read PDFs with a fast local viewer
• Merge, split, rotate, and reorder pages
• Compress PDFs to shrink file size
• OCR scanned pages (on-device)
• Sign documents (drawn / typed signatures; digital IDs where supported)
• Annotate and organize documents in one place

Privacy
• Core processing stays on your phone or tablet
• No sign-in required for core features
• Your files are not uploaded to Document Studio servers

Document Studio is built for people who want capable PDF tools without sending sensitive documents to the cloud.
```
