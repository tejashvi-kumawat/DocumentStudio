## Title

Privacy Policy — Document Studio

## Summary

Document Studio processes PDFs and images **on your device**. Core features do not require an account or internet. We do not upload your documents to Document Studio servers.

## Data we process on device

- Documents and images you open or create
- App settings, recent-file list, and locally saved signatures / digital IDs
- Temporary working files in app storage (cleaned after jobs / on restart where applicable)

## Data we do not collect (core product)

- No account registration
- No cloud OCR / cloud conversion of your files
- No sale of personal data
- No advertising ID–based ads in the core product

## Sharing

We do not share your documents with third parties. If you use the system **Share** sheet, you choose the destination app — that is outside Document Studio’s servers.

## Optional network

If a future feature downloads language packs or updates with your explicit action, only that download traffic occurs; document contents are not sent. A strict offline mode (if enabled in-app) blocks even those downloads.
